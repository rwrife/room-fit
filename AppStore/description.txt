Room Fit — plan room layouts at real scale, offline.

Enter room dimensions, add doors and windows, and arrange furniture with measured sizes. See approximate furniture fit, door swings, and walkway clearances. Duplicate and rename layouts to compare ideas; export or import your layouts as a local JSON file. Works on Windows, Android phones and tablets, and iPhone and iPad with platform-adapted controls.

No account, analytics, camera scanning, or cloud sync. Check measurements and actual fit in person before buying or moving furniture. Clearance estimates are planning aids, not building-code or accessibility compliance claims.
